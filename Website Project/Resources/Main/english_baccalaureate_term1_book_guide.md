# English Book Guide — Egyptian Baccalaureate, Second Year
## First Term | 2026–2027

### Scope and shared syllabus

The six uploaded documents cover the same first-term English course. The **official Student Book** defines the curriculum; the external books explain it, extend practice, provide revision, and add novel or language-skill material.

All five main-course units follow the same syllabus:

1. **Living Well in a Complex World (Well-being):** social media and self-image, modern lifestyles, responsibility, and mental health.
2. **Leisure Time (Experiences):** screen time, creative arts, sport, physical activity, and leisure experiences.
3. **Echoes of the Past (Identity):** historical sites, museums, viral history, and social media and historical events.
4. **The Power of Choice (Consumption and Choice):** food waste, brands, digital consumption, and Black Friday culture.
5. **The Greenhouse Effect (Sharing the Planet):** climate change, renewable energy, greening projects in Egypt, and individual and collective action.

The main difference between the books is their **teaching and practice approach**, rather than the topics they cover.

---

## 1. Official Student Book — Part One

**Type:** The Ministry of Education's core curriculum and primary reference.

**What it does:** Establishes the learning goals, themes, inquiry questions, and skills for the term. Its approach is inquiry-based and concept-driven: students are expected to understand, analyse, discuss, evaluate, and communicate ideas instead of memorising fixed answers. The introduction explains the **JRE+ model**: Justification, Reasoning, Evidence, followed by reflection and real-world application.

**Structure:** Five concept-based units. Each has two sections, two lessons per section, and a unit assessment. Lessons integrate reading, listening, speaking, vocabulary, grammar in context, discussion, and writing. Reflection and self-assessment tasks appear throughout. Two consolidation reviews cover Units 1–2 and Units 3–4.

**Best use:** Follow it first. It is the reference for the official syllabus and the type of thinking and skills expected in class and assessment.

## 2. El-Moasser — Main Guide (2027)

**Type:** A detailed external study guide that follows the official five-unit syllabus.

**What it does:** Explains the lessons and gives guided practice in vocabulary, grammar, reading, listening, and writing. Its guidebook introduces a clear study sequence: **Explore the Idea** (respond to the inquiry question), **Build Your Language** (study word meaning, form, word families, collocations, context, and grammar), then read in stages—get the message, find evidence, and think beyond the text. It also explains command words such as *identify, explain, analyse, compare, evaluate,* and *justify*.

**Structure:** The five units are divided into Section A and Section B, with four lessons per unit. Lessons combine vocabulary and grammar work, texts, comprehension and analysis questions, writing guidance, and unit assessments. The guide also promotes Scan & Listen/audio vocabulary support and companion assessment resources.

**Best use:** Step-by-step lesson study, deeper vocabulary and grammar practice, and learning to extend a JRE answer with explanation and another point of view.

## 3. El-Moasser — Novel and Pre-Assessment Supplement

**Type:** A combined novel guide and preparatory skills booklet.

**What it does and contains:** The novel section covers Chapters 1–5 of Arthur Conan Doyle's *The Lost World*. It starts with an overview of the writer, setting, plot, themes, and characters. Each chapter is supported by vocabulary, a summary in points, the chapter text, comprehension and analytical questions, and JRE-style model answers.

The same uploaded file also contains a **Pre-Assessment** section. Its four parts cover scenario-based vocabulary in context, reading comprehension, writing skills, and a consolidation review. It explains how to approach the question types and apply the required skills rather than simply memorising answers.

**Best use:** First-five-chapter novel study and preparatory practice in vocabulary, reading, and writing.

## 4. Giants — “As the Exam Says” (Part One)

**Type:** An intensive revision and question-practice book.

**What it does:** Covers all five units through vocabulary, texts, question banks, and grammar. It opens with a guide to common instruction words, the **JRE (Justify–Reason–Evidence)** method, and an **Idea + Why** approach for explaining answers.

**Structure:** Unit material includes vocabulary in context, definitions, word families, collocations, expressions, synonyms and antonyms, reading texts, comprehension and analytical questions, grammar practice, writing tasks, and general unit exercises. Questions marked **[SB] / [SBR]** are identified as Student Book questions. It also contains a simplified *The Lost World* section for Chapters 1–5, with vocabulary, story points, text, and chapter questions.

**Best use:** Intensive exam-style practice, vocabulary revision, grammar reinforcement, and repeated JRE training.

## 5. GEM — Main Book (2027)

**Type:** A structured external course companion.

**What it does:** Follows the same five units and develops the Baccalaureate focus on skills, critical thinking, vocabulary and grammar in context, and JRE-based answers.

**Structure:** Each unit has Section A, Section B, and a skills/general-exercises section. Lesson work repeatedly includes key and main vocabulary, vocabulary checkpoints, phrases and prepositions, derivatives, synonyms and antonyms, reading or listening texts, comprehension questions, grammar checkpoints, and grammar practice. JRE questions appear throughout, with suggested answers collected towards the end. Two consolidation reviews cover Units 1–2 and Units 3–4.

**Best use:** Organised lesson-by-lesson study and progress checks, especially for vocabulary, grammar accuracy, and evidence-based reading answers.

## 6. GEM — Novel and Skills Booklet Supplement

**Type:** A separate novel companion plus a four-part language-skills booklet.

**Novel structure:** Introduces Arthur Conan Doyle, the story, setting, and characters. Chapter material includes vocabulary, expressions and prepositions, chapter points, the story text, comprehension and analytical questions, JRE model answers, and chapter exercises.

**Skills Booklet structure:**

1. **Reading Comprehension:** methods for fiction and non-fiction passages, locating evidence, and answering multiple-choice, open-ended, and true/false-with-justification questions.
2. **Writing and Punctuation:** paragraph structure, different writing forms, punctuation guidance, examples, and exercises.
3. **Translation:** practice from English into Arabic and from Arabic into English.
4. **Dialog:** forming Yes/No and WH- questions, with dialogue practice.

**Important first-term note:** The uploaded GEM supplement's contents list **Chapters 1–10**. The Moasser and Giants first-term novel materials list only **Chapters 1–5**. Therefore, Chapters 1–5 are the shared range across the uploaded first-term materials; Chapters 6–10 are additional content in the GEM file, and the uploaded sources do not establish whether those extra chapters are required for the first-term assessment.

**Best use:** Chapter-by-chapter novel study and focused practice in reading, writing, translation, and dialogue construction.

---

## How the resources fit together

- **Official Student Book:** the curriculum anchor—what to study and the skills to develop.
- **El-Moasser Main Guide:** detailed explanation and guided practice.
- **Giants:** intensive revision and question-bank practice.
- **GEM Main Book:** structured explanations, frequent vocabulary and grammar checks, and JRE models.
- **El-Moasser Supplement:** first-five-chapter novel support plus pre-assessment preparation.
- **GEM Supplement:** novel support plus a separate booklet for the four language-skill areas.

**Overall:** These are complementary resources for one first-term syllabus, not six different syllabuses. Use the official Student Book as the reference, then choose the external guides and supplements for explanation, practice, revision, and skills development.
